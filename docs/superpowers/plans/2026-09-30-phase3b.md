## Review focus
- Legacy hidden items must require a new final correct result, including after replay/import.
- Discard/delete of historical test removes contribution without disturbing dismissal.
- Training inventory and answer snapshots never rebind on refresh or source changes.
- Training drafts and result saves never overwrite ordinary free-practice state.
- Navigation cannot race saves or silently create a fresh run.
